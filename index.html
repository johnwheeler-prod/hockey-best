Criterion 2

Physicality of Play

  • NHL: ~40–50+ combined hits per game
  • NHL hits average 35g peak acceleration vs. NFL's 29.2g
  • That's ~17% harder, despite NHL players being nearly 20% smaller
  • Physics: skating velocity at contact exceeds running velocity
  • NBA / MLB: no meaningful full-body contact as a core game mechanic

* This methodology of measuring physicality has critics. Noted. Moving on.

Criterion 3

Hardest Skills Required

ESPN rated 60 sports across 10 athletic skill categories.

#2 of 60

Ice Hockey. Behind only boxing. Ahead of football, basketball, and baseball.

Baseball fans will note: hitting a baseball is widely considered the hardest single skill in all of sports.
95mph fastball. 400ms to the plate. 150ms to decide. Half-inch margin of error. A .300 average is considered excellent.
That is one genuinely extraordinary skill.

Baseball has one.
Hockey requires you to skate at elite speed, stickhandle at full stride, execute precise passes and shots, and simultaneously deliver and absorb hits at 35g.
Every shift. The ESPN panel agreed.

* One caveat: I'd argue durability is underweighted. A boxing match runs 36 minutes, contested a handful of times per year. An NHL player plays 82 regular season games plus up to 28 playoff games — 60 minutes of full-contact hockey each. Durability deserves a higher share of this skill matrix.

Why Hockey is Actually Special

The Speed

Hockey players skate at 20–25 mph, sustained in traffic, while reading a puck moving at 100 mph and tracking everyone on the ice. The ice removes the cost of deceleration. You can change direction at full speed. No other team sport operates at this tempo.

The Duality

Hockey asks for physicality and finesse from the same player, in the same shift, on the same sequence of play. The person who just absorbed a 35g hit will, five seconds later, thread a no-look pass through traffic. Both things are the game.

The Handshake Line

After weeks of hitting the same people in a 7-game playoff series, both teams line up and every player shakes hands with every opponent. Genuinely. Mandatory by culture alone. Unique to professional sports.

Game 7 Overtime

Sixty minutes couldn't separate you. Twenty more minutes of sudden death couldn't either. One shot. One bounce. One pass read slightly wrong, and it ends, instantly, completely. And you know it the moment it happens because the sound in the building will change your understanding of what sound can be.

Objections

Counterarguments,
Addressed and Dismissed

"Hockey isn't popular in warm climates."
5 of the last 6 Stanley Cup champions are from warm-weather markets.
2020: Tampa Bay. 2021: Tampa Bay. 2023: Las Vegas. 2024: Florida. 2025: Florida.
Vegas Golden Knights: first-season attendance 103% of capacity, sold out every home game, had a 2,500-person waitlist for year-two season tickets before year one ended.
Florida leads the country in youth hockey enrollment growth. Someone forgot to tell the trophy.
"The NHL has a smaller fanbase than the NFL or NBA."
The NHL is the fastest-growing major sport right now. See Criterion 4. The NBA is down 50% since 2011. The NHL is up 40% this season alone. We can revisit this argument in five years.
"2–1 games are boring. Not enough scoring."
Baseball averages 4–3 and people watch men stand in a field for three and a half hours. A 2–1 hockey game means both goalies were standing on their heads making impossible saves for 60 minutes. Also, see Criterion 1, Overtime edition, where players are eating pizza between periods to stay conscious.
